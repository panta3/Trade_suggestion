\begin{infobox}
\textbf{System Overview} --- Two parallel strategies run fully automated via Windows Task Scheduler.
The \textbf{Swing algo} scans 65+ symbols on daily bars for multi-day momentum trades (hold 3--15 days).
The \textbf{Intraday algo} scans the same universe on 5-min bars for same-day CALL/PUT signals (hold minutes to hours).
Both log paper trades to JSON files and validate P\&L nightly. Push notifications fire on Grade~S/A signals via ntfy.sh.
\end{infobox}

\section{Weekly Manual Checklist (5 min every Friday)}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em, label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
  \item \textbf{Read the weekly review output} --- check validate.log or re-run:
        \texttt{bash scripts/weekly\_review.sh}
  \item \textbf{Check the threshold recommendation} from \texttt{scripts/optimize\_threshold.py}.
        If it suggests a new threshold, update \texttt{day\_trading.py}: \texttt{SIGNAL\_THRESHOLD = XX}
  \item \textbf{Scan for any Grade S/A signals} you may have missed. Open the web dashboard
        (\texttt{python3 api.py} then \texttt{http://localhost:5000}).
  \item \textbf{Review swing trades} still open: check \texttt{data/paper\_trades.json} for trades
        open longer than 15 days --- consider manually closing.
  \item \textbf{Review intraday accuracy}: \texttt{data/accuracy\_stats.json} --- track win rate trend
        week over week.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Universe \& Pre-Screener}

The scanner starts with \textbf{65 symbols} across 9 sectors. Before full evaluation, a parallel
pre-screener fetches relative volume for all symbols in $\sim$3 seconds and keeps only the
\textbf{top 45 by relative volume} (minimum rel-vol 0.6$\times$). This reduces noise and focuses
compute on the most active names each session.

\textbf{Sectors covered:} Index ETFs (SPY, QQQ, IWM, TQQQ) --- Mega-cap tech (TSLA, NVDA, AAPL, MSFT, META, GOOGL, AMZN) ---
Semiconductors (NVDA, AMD, INTC, QCOM, MU) --- Financials (JPM, GS, COIN) ---
Energy (XOM, CVX, OXY) --- Healthcare (LLY, MRNA) --- Consumer/Internet (UBER, ABNB, ROKU) ---
High-beta / Speculative (MARA, RIOT, PLTR, SOFI, RIVN, UPST).

\section{Next 3--4 Weeks: What to Track}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em, label=\textbf{Week \arabic*:}]
  \item \textbf{Validate data pipeline.} Confirm validate.log is updating at 4:15 PM each day.
        Check that closed trade P\&Ls make sense (not all 0\%). Monitor ntfy alerts.
  \item \textbf{First performance read.} After 20+ closed intraday trades, check win rate and profit factor.
        Run \texttt{optimize\_threshold.py} --- adjust threshold if recommended.
  \item \textbf{Grade breakdown.} Separate Grade S/A from Grade B trades. If S/A WR $\geq 60\%$,
        the high-conviction filter is working.
  \item \textbf{Decision point.} With 50+ closed trades: does the algo have a confirmed edge?
        If WR $> 55\%$ and PF $> 1.3$ --- consider whether to adjust position sizing or universe.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{warnbox}
\textbf{Remember:} The intraday backtest on hourly bars showed no edge because hourly bars $\neq$ 5-min intraday bars.
The live paper trade data over the next 3--4 weeks is the real signal. Do not tune the algo
based on fewer than 30 closed trades --- that is not enough data to be statistically meaningful.
\end{warnbox}
